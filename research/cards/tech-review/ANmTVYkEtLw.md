## 8. Retention mechanics: why
- **The skit builds identity, at the cost of tension.** It turns a spec war into an image and
  names the title. But for 73 seconds a viewer who came for a review gets no review
  information (inferred). The job is brand identity.
- **An argument frame.** The problem at 1:32, the evidence (chip, charging, cameras), the
  callback at 8:36, then the verdict. This clarifies and builds tension.
- **Grades and charts.** "B+ camera" and the benchmark bars let a skimming viewer take away
  the result. This is the job of clarity and proof.
- **A bookend payoff.** The poker table returns for the last line, with its music. This
  shows a payoff.
- What this video knows: graphics compress specs well. What it risks: the cold open delays
  the promise.
