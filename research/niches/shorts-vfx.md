## Shared devices: the house style (seen unless marked)
- **Zero hard cuts.** Each Short plays as one handheld take. Any joins sit inside whips,
  swings and blurred turns (inferred).
- **The creator performs on camera,** usually as the one the trick happens to.
- **Practical-looking effects in real places:** a wall, a table, a garden. Natural light and
  no visible grade.
- **No text over the effect.** At most a 1.5-second opening question.
- **11-26 seconds, one premise,** ending on the payoff frame with no outro.
- **Dynamic sound:** LRA 8.4-14.2 LU, the widest in this batch, with the 2-second loudness
  swinging 9.6-14.1 LU inside each Short (measured).

- **H1: the change must happen on camera.** Both outliers show a transformation; the
  control shows a finished impossible state. Watching it happen invites checking and
  rewatching.
- **H2: borrow a reference the whole feed knows.** A famous artwork or a familiar trend
  gives an instant hook with no words.
- **H3: a calm camera sells the trick.** The outliers move less (0.68-0.79 against 1.34).
- **H4: ask or claim, don't label.** "Illusion" tells the viewer the answer before the
  video starts.
- The outliers' low likes per view (about a third of the control's) suggests their reach
  went far beyond the creator's audience (inferred).
- Upload age: 5jhEoRZXhjE is 2 months older than the control, X0wxLr5W2X8 is 6 weeks
  younger, so age doesn't explain the pattern.

- **Measured (5jhEoRZXhjE only):** the last 6 seconds turn dark and bassy, brightness

  caption [music] tag sits at 13.1s as the guard appears.
- **Inferred:** sound here is event design, not a bed under talk: a build into an impact,
  a drop for the poetic payoff, silence around a comic reveal. These map onto the chapter's
  silence devices, "sound drop before a reveal" and "hard cut to silence"
  (`knowledge/sound-design.md` §2.2), and onto riser-into-impact for the crash
  (`knowledge/music-and-mood.md` §3.4). The chapters' VFX-Shorts claim that the music drives
  the edit can't be tested here: there are no cuts.
- **Music measured** for X0wxLr5W2X8 and tOIFZ8O6Ezk: tempo 103.4 BPM

## Steal list
| Technique | Measured range | Works when | Backfires when | HyperFrames recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One-take look | 0 hard cuts in 11-26s | magic, transformations, reveals | information content that needs cutaways | one continuous scene; hide any swap in a 4-6f blurred move (`filter: blur(0→20px→0)` across the swap) |
| Transformation on camera | 1-3 per Short, the first by 2-7s | anything flat that can become real, or real that can turn out fake | the swap frame is visible | two layers (before, after), swapped at the blur peak; `clip-path` reveal if the change spreads |
| Borrowed icon or trend in 2-3s | famous image recognised by ~2s; trend question spoken at 0.0s | broad, cold audiences | the reference is niche, or the use isn't allowed | open on the icon with a pull-back, `scale 2.2→1` over 3s `sine.inOut` |
| Calm camera around the trick | visual energy 0.68-0.79 | the trick must read in one look | action scenes that need energy | slow `sine.inOut` pushes only; no shake within 1s of the reveal frame |
| Guess-along question | spoken at 0.0s, captioned by 2.0s, then no text | tricks with a visible answer | an obvious answer | word-by-word caption for the question only, removed by 2s |
| Sound shaped to the action | 9.6-14.1 LU swing; LRA 8.4-14.2 | builds, falls, escapes | talk-led Shorts, where the voice must stay level | gain automation on the audio group: a build into the impact frame, then a −8 to −10 LU drop with a low-pass for a poetic ending |
