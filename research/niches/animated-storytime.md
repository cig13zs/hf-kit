# Niche DNA: animated storytime

Three JaidenAnimations videos, checked 2026-09-26. Cards: `cards/animated-storytime/`.
- plSyrHqUh78, "I Attempted my First Pokemon Nuzlocke": long-form outlier, 7.14×, 17:49
- Oc91iOxcB3c, "I got a cat.": recent outlier, 3.0×, 16:03
- OQx7q6mwkV4, "My First Time Playing DUNGEONS & DRAGONS": control, 1.0×, 17:43

All three come from one channel, so this sheet describes one creator's house style. It is hand
animation, so HyperFrames can reproduce the editing, type, UI and camera grammar, but the
drawings themselves must be made first (original art, frame-by-frame or rigged).

## Shared devices (all three: the house style)
- **A talking-head cut rate on drawings.** 21.6-23.3 cuts/min, median shot 1.88-2.0s, and
  18.5-24.8 cuts/min in every segment after the first 15s (measured). A hand-drawn channel cuts
  like a vlog.
- **Fast first-person narration from the first frame.** First word at 0.03-0.04s and 194-222
  wpm in the first 30s (measured). Every video opens with Jaiden's avatar on white explaining
  the premise to camera (seen in all three hook sheets).
- **Black line art on white, colour for the subject.** Pokémon and the red cap, the cat's
  orange patches and yellow eyes, the painted fantasy world (seen). Rooms and flashbacks are
  often a flat lavender-grey wash (seen in Oc91iOxcB3c and OQx7q6mwkV4).
- **Punch-ins and reaction close-ups on still drawings** (seen: a deadpan close-up at 4:13.9 in
  plSyrHqUh78, a push-in at 6:47 in Oc91iOxcB3c, a push-in on the door's face at 4:55 in
  OQx7q6mwkV4).
- **Hard cuts only.** No whip, glitch, shake transition or speed ramp in any of the 36 sampled
  cut pairs (seen). Energy comes from speed lines and impact frames inside a shot.
- **A slow last minute:** 6-10 cuts/min under credits that name the team or the patrons
  ("Inbetweeners"; "Support the team") (measured and seen).
- **Very little music on the captions:** 0.06-0.34 [music] tags per minute and a bass share
  median of 0.05-0.13 (measured). See the sound profile.

## Outlier against control (every causal claim is a hypothesis)
| Dimension | Outliers (plSyrHqUh78, Oc91iOxcB3c) | Control (OQx7q6mwkV4) |
|---|---|---|
| Protagonist | Jaiden herself, start to finish | invented characters played by her and guest players after 0:56 |
| Attachment | a team she nicknamed, which dies one by one; a real cat she waited for | a comic party with no losses |
| Proof of the real | the game's own UI recreated; real photos and video of the cat | none; a fiction |
| Hook | rules and a past failure (16s); two viewer objections (23s) | a premise and a friend's anecdote |
| Cuts/min, median shot | 21.6-23.3, 1.88-2.0s | 22.0, 1.88s |
| LUFS, LRA | −15.2 to −15.9, 2.2-4.1 LU | −12.9, 6.8 LU |
| Cuts on onsets | 1.22-1.23 | 1.02 |
| Silences/min | 1.52 and 0.19 (they disagree) | 0.11 |

Hypotheses:
1. **The creator must be the protagonist.** Both outliers are about something that happened to
   Jaiden. The control hands the story to a party of invented characters after one minute. The
   cut rate and pace are identical, so they are not the difference.
2. **Attachment the viewer can feel.** The Nuzlocke's own rule (nicknames "to get more attached")
   is the video's engine, and the cat video spends eight minutes of setbacks before the kitten
   comes home. The control's characters have jokes but nothing to lose.
3. **The title's form does not separate them.** All three titles are first-person ("I attempted",
   "I got", "My first time"). What follows the "I" does.
4. **Cuts on sound, weakly.** Both outliers land cuts on audio onsets 1.22-1.23× chance; the
   control sits at chance (1.02). That may mean a sound hit on more cuts, but with two videos it
   is a lead, not a finding.
5. **Silence is not the shared driver.** The Nuzlocke uses 1.52 silences/min, the cat video 0.19.

## Sound and music profile
- **Level:** −15.2 to −15.9 LUFS in the outliers, −12.9 in the control (measured). The control
  sits above YouTube's −14 reference, so the platform turns it down about 1 dB (inferred from
  the knowledge chapter's normalisation rule).
- **Range:** 2.2-4.1 LU in the outliers, 6.8 LU in the control (measured). One narrator makes a
  flat mix; a cast of voices and fight effects makes a wide one (inferred).
- **A thin bed, voice first:** bass share medians of 0.05-0.13 against 0.21-0.27 in the animated
  explainers, and 0.06-0.34 [music] tags per minute against 3.3-6.5 (measured). Music is light,
  sparse or both (inferred). In the Nuzlocke the bass share rises to 0.14-0.25 only where the
  voice drops out (2:30-2:44, 3:20-3:30, 4:10), so the low end arrives in the pauses (measured
  windows; the reading is inferred).
- **Silence and dips as punctuation, done two ways.** The Nuzlocke puts real silences (0.4-2.2s)
  on deaths and reversals, 1.52/min (measured). The control has almost no silences (0.11/min)
  but drops 8-22 LU under its median about nine times, three of them within 2s of a [music]
  tag, so the voices stop and music plays alone (measured timings; inferred reading). The cat
  video has one 10 LU dip, on a gag at 0:46 (measured).
- **Outros:** the Nuzlocke's credits sit 10-15 LU under the body (−25 to −31 LUFS); the cat
  video's last 20s is its loudest stretch (−13.6 to −11.9) (measured).
- **Tempo** estimates are weak in all three (confidence 0.97-1.62) and not usable (measured).
- This only partly matches the music chapter's animated-storytime row (a bed under the VO,
  music-out before punchlines). The numbers suggest less bed than that row assumes.

## Steal list
| Technique | Measured range | Works when | Backfires when | HyperFrames recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Talking-head cut rate on drawings | 21.6-23.3 cuts/min, median shot 1.88-2.0s | a narrator-led story with many small beats | an explainer that needs time to read a diagram | one sub-composition per drawn shot, `data-duration` 1.5-2.5s; alternate wide and close of the same art with `tl.set(art,{scale:1.3,transformOrigin:"55% 40%"})` on alternate cuts |
| Stakes in the first 20s | 3 rules in 16s; 2 objections in 23s | a challenge, or a title that surprises the audience | the setup runs past 30s | vignettes on one white ground, each `from({y:40,autoAlpha:0})` 300 ms `back.out(1.5)` on its first word; exits `y:-30` 200 ms `power2.in`; no hard cut until the premise is stated |
| Event, then reaction | about 1 reaction cut per event beat | comedy with a narrator-avatar | the reaction repeats the same face every time | two shots per beat: the event (1.5-3s), then an avatar close-up (0.6-1.2s) as its own clip on the next track; 3-4 expression drawings rotated |
| Loss card with silence | 0.4-2.2s silences, one loss every 1-3 min | a named character the viewer knows | a trivial loss | pause the scene's child timeline; `filter:grayscale(1)` over 300 ms; card `scale:1.3→1`, 150 ms `power4.out`; bed volume lane `{"t":T,"v":0}` to `{"t":T+1.2,"v":0}`, back on the next line |
| Colour only on the subject | line art on white; one colour family per subject | a character or creature the story is about | everything is coloured, so nothing pops | SVG line art `stroke:#111;fill:#fff`; the subject `fill:var(--hero)`; flashback wash as a `#d9d6ea` layer in `mix-blend-mode:multiply` |
| Real photos as proof | one grid mid-video plus credits | a real pet, place or object behind the drawing | photos that don't match the design | 2×3 CSS grid, each `<img>` `from({scale:0.9,autoAlpha:0})` 250 ms `power3.out`, 80 ms stagger; a drawn sticker on top at `rotation:-6` |
| Callback at the end | a 9-name roll call; both hook objections answered at 13:51 and 14:38 | a long video that set up names or doubts | the setup was never clear | reuse the setup's sub-composition as a 0.4-1s insert right before the payoff line |
| Voice-first thin bed | bass share 0.05-0.13; [music] tags ≤0.34/min | a single fast narrator at 169-190 wpm | a quiet, slow story that needs mood | bed at `data-volume="0.08"` with a volume lane that lifts it to `v:1` only in pauses over 0.8s; high-pass the bed so the voice owns the low mids |
