## Shared devices (all three: the house style)
- **A superlative in the first second over the finished thing.** "the world's first ever
  actual pool of Jell-O", "the most dangerous robot I've ever built", "the most epic birthday
  party ever", each spoken at 0.0-0.2s while the finished build is on screen (seen in
  transcripts and hook sheets).
- **Fast talk in the first 30 seconds:** 178-220 wpm (measured), well above each video's
  average.
- **No captions and almost no text:** 0-3% of frames (measured). The pictures and the voice
  carry everything.
- **Hard cuts and practical spectacle.** Fire, smoke, snow, sparks and Jell-O are real. No
  designed transitions (whip, glitch, zoom-through) appeared in any cut pair (seen).
- **Physics in plain words at the moment it matters:** convection "like when you blow on the
  top of a hot cup of soup" (DPZzrlFCD_I 3:46), a steel-cable "mustache" bayonet (P4gNS0Iiu0Q
  2:08), dummy tests and thrust vectors (9IiYOTzJ2uw 1:29-3:01) (seen in transcripts).
- **Pop-culture and crossover inserts:** Phineas and Ferb in two videos, The Office, and Dude
  Perfect (seen).
- **A closing gag, then an ad or end card** (seen in all three endings).

## Outlier against control (every causal claim is a hypothesis)
| Dimension | Outliers (DPZzrlFCD_I, P4gNS0Iiu0Q) | Control (9IiYOTzJ2uw) |
|---|---|---|
| Premise | one sentence: a pool of Jell-O; one robot against five | several parts: a party, a sick kid, a cartoon, three builds |
| Stakes stated by | 0:21 (the belly-flop question); 0:15-0:36 (5 v 1, no rules, a forfeit) | 4:55-5:22 (the kid's illness) |
| Cut pace | 19.7 and 47.9 cuts/min | 41.2 cuts/min (between them) |
| Integrated loudness | −11.5 and −7.5 LUFS | −13.8 LUFS |
| Beat-cut montage | yes in DPZzrlFCD_I (lift 1.31) | no (lift 1.03) |

Hypotheses:
1. **Pace is not the lever.** The two outliers sit on either side of the control's cut rate.
2. **A one-line premise travels.** Both outliers can be said in under ten words; the control
   needs a paragraph. A premise that is easy to repeat may be easier to click and share.
3. **Stakes before 0:40.** Both outliers state what could go wrong, or what is at stake, in the
   first 36 seconds. The control's emotional stake waits five minutes.
4. **A louder payoff.** In DPZzrlFCD_I the payoff minutes are ~4 LU louder than the narration
   and beat-cut (measured). The control's loudest moment is its opening montage, and its cuts
   don't follow a beat.

## Sound and music profile
- **Level:** −7.5 to −13.8 LUFS integrated (measured), all at or above the −14 reference.
  YouTube turns the louder ones down (knowledge chapter), so the difference that survives
  playback is density and range, not volume (inferred).
- **Two mixing philosophies:** a wide-range story mix (DPZzrlFCD_I, LRA 9.7 LU) where narration
  is quiet and the payoff montage jumps ~4 LU, and a wall-of-sound versus mix (P4gNS0Iiu0Q, LRA
  1.7 LU, one silence in 19 minutes) (measured). The control sits between them (6.7 LU).
- **Silence and dips before reveals:** a 0.55s silence right before the Jell-O tarp reveal
  (DPZzrlFCD_I 4:30) and a dip to −22/−26 before the kid's surprise (9IiYOTzJ2uw 10:04)
  (measured, transcript). This matches the chapter's "tension bed, then silence at the outcome"
  row for this genre. The versus video skips it (measured: no silences).
- **Music takes over for payoffs:** transcript gaps with no narration line up with montages
  (DPZzrlFCD_I 2:29-3:13, 5:00-6:58, 8:41-9:45), and bass share rises to 0.43-0.57 in those
  minutes (measured). Only there do cuts land on onsets above chance (lift 1.31, measured).
- **Dialogue elsewhere:** outside montages the cut follows speech and action (lift 1.03 in both
  the control and the robot outlier, measured), even at 48 cuts per minute.
- **Brightness:** 877-1357 Hz median (measured); brighter than the documentary niche's
  742-825 Hz, consistent with more voices, kids, crowds and outdoor sound (inferred).

## Steal list
| Technique | Measured range | Works when | Backfires when | HyperFrames recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Superlative over the finished object | first word at 0.0-0.2s; 178-220 wpm in the first 30s | the build looks good in one frame | the claim is unverifiable or the object underwhelms | the hero clip starts at `data-start="0"`, VO starts on frame 0, no title card |
| Payoff first, then "N months ago" | payoff at 0:00; delayed payoff action at ~50% of runtime | builds and tests with a clear end state | the middle has no tension of its own | a 3-15s payoff montage, then a hard cut to an empty-space still with a date label |
| Proof that others failed | 3-4 clips in ~5s | "first ever" or "done right" claims | it punches down at named small creators | a screen-recorded search, then 1s clips with source watermarks kept |
| Test, fail, fix with slow-mo | one loop per 30-90s | engineering and physics | failures look staged | pre-rendered 0.25× replay; an arrow SVG drawn onto the failure point over 300 ms |
| Payoff montage: fewer cuts, louder music | 7 cuts/min against 16-32; +4 LU; lift 1.31 | visually satisfying action | dull or repetitive action | 4-8s clips in a music-only sub-composition cut on bar lines; music `volume` +4 dB; VO off |
| Doubt, silence, reveal | ~30s of doubt; 0.5s silence; reveal | real risk of failure | the outcome was never in doubt | music `volume:0` 0.5s before the reveal clip; music back on its first downbeat |
| Versus with stakes and a bracket | stakes by 0:36; 5 rounds of 2-4 min | rivals with visible reactions | rounds feel identical | a reusable round-intro card; alternate impact clips with 0.6-1.2s reaction clips |
