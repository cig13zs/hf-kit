## Shared devices: the house style (seen unless marked)
- **The creator's face, always on screen,** against a bright green backdrop, full frame or
  in the top third.
- Social posts and app screenshots as the content. Every video shows its source within
  the first 2.2 seconds.
- **Condensed bold uppercase captions,** white with a dark outline, 2-4 words, one word in a
  highlight colour (cyan or yellow).
- **Wall-to-wall speech** from 0.0s at 215-305 wpm, with no silence over 0.4s (measured).
- **12-15 seconds, one idea,** ending on the last informative line with no outro or call to
  action.
- Flat, compressed mix: LRA 1.0-2.4 LU (measured).
- No VFX. No zooms, whips, shakes, stickers or transitions beyond hard swaps.

- H1: a concrete claim in sentence one beats a teaser. The only first-line difference
  that tracks views.
- H2: breadth of stake. The outliers' topics touch most of the audience; the control's
  touches a paying minority.
- H3: show the result. Both outliers put a payoff image on screen; the control shows
  promotional art.
- Pace, loudness and layout don't separate them: the control is the fastest-cut video.
- The outliers' lower likes per view suggests their extra views came from beyond the core
  audience (inferred).

## Sound and music profile
- Measured: −12.1 to −14.9 LUFS; LRA 1.0-2.4 LU, the flattest mixes in this batch; zero
  silences; onsets 90-130/min.
- Measured pattern: onsets rise with speech rate in all three (215 wpm → 90, 264 → 104,
  305 → 130 onsets/min). With 1-4 cuts per video, the onsets aren't cut hits. Speech
  syllables are the likeliest driver (inferred). Three points make a hint, not a law.
- Inferred: a voice-first mix with either no bed or a very low one. The knowledge
  chapters' gaming-Shorts row (hype or phonk, drop on the highlight) doesn't apply to this
  talk format. The podcast-clip row fits better: speech-led, bed absent or low
  (`knowledge/music-and-mood.md` §5).
- Designed SFX density is unknown: onsets can't separate SFX from syllables here.
- **Music block** for all three: tempo 95.7-129.2 BPM (measured); no
  [music] caption tags; bass share 0.03-0.12 (measured). No evidence of a bed.
  Cut-on-onset lift is not computable for any of the three (measured).

## Steal list
| Technique | Measured range | Works when | Backfires when | HyperFrames recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claim in sentence one | full claim spoken from 0.0s (outliers) | news, how-to, anything with a concrete stake | the claim needs a setup to make sense | first caption group = the claim's noun phrase; voice `data-start="0"` |
| Source by 2 seconds | post on screen at 0-2.2s | claims viewers might doubt | a weak source that invites "fake" comments | screenshot scene at ≤1.9s; `scale 0.96→1` 0.3s `power2.out` |
| Face-banner-content stack | face ~30-33% top, banner ~35%, content below | creator-led news | footage that needs the full frame | three stacked divs in the 1080×1920 root; the face wrapper stays for the whole runtime |
| Karaoke captions | 2-4 words, active word coloured, 215-305 wpm | fast talk | slow or emotional delivery | per-word spans; `tl.set(span,{color:'#3be8ff'}, t)` at each word's start |
| Read-along highlight | 4 steps in 6s (2n1rIXEeybM) | reading a post, law or headline aloud | long passages | spans over the source text; background colour set per phrase at the word timestamps |
| Result on screen | payoff visible at 7-9s of 12 | tutorials and news with a visible outcome | the outcome isn't visual | a screen-recording clip placed at ~60% of runtime, no caption over its key area |
| Stop on the last fact | 0s of outro in all three | Shorts under 20s | long stories that need a close | no end scene; the last caption group ends the timeline |
| Gapless fast talk | 0 silences, LRA 1.0-2.4 LU | information Shorts | jokes that need a beat of silence | tighten voice gaps to under 0.4s in the edit; one compressor on the voice bus |
