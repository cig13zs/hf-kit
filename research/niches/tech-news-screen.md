## Shared devices (all three: the house style)
- **Voice on frame one, never stopping.** First word at 0.0s, 202-210 wpm, and no silence over
  0.4s anywhere before the final second (measured).
- **A 43-50s cold open that ends on a fixed show line** naming the date and "The Code Report", then
  a title card: a white-outlined square with a yellow date tag, which can carry a joke date (seen
  in aspmNhKAFMc and E7la7-dtfVM; the line is in all three transcripts).
- **A black canvas with floating screenshots.** Pages, tweets, papers and dashboards sit on black
  as cards, often tilted, and pile up as items are listed (seen in all three).
- **Emphasis kit:** orange or red highlight bars on the quoted line, hand-drawn white arrows,
  red arrows and underlines (seen in all three).
- **Sticker captions:** heavy condensed all caps, black on a coloured box or white/yellow with a
  thick black outline, tilted 5-15°, landing on the spoken word (seen in all three).
- **Memes and borrowed clips on punchlines.** Each video inserts at least one clip that plays its
  own audio (`>>` markers: FluKUJyeYD8 0:58.8, aspmNhKAFMc 0:27.2 and 4:45.7, E7la7-dtfVM 3:50),
  and that clip is the video's loudest moment (measured) or near it.
- **Pixel-block dissolve** at topic turns, never on every cut (seen in all three).
- **No camera moves or flashy transitions.** No whips, zoom-throughs, shakes or light leaks in any
  sampled frame (seen).
- **A flat, quiet mix:** −20.3 to −23.8 LUFS, LRA 2.4-3.2 LU (measured).
- **Sponsor at the end, then a fixed sign-off** and an end card with a tilted offer sticker and a
  "Link below" pointer (seen in all three).
- **Last minute slows to 2-8 cuts** (measured): the sponsor screen recording and end card.

## Outlier against control (every causal claim is a hypothesis)
| Dimension | Outliers (FluKUJyeYD8, aspmNhKAFMc) | Control (E7la7-dtfVM) |
|---|---|---|
| Topic | a famous AI lab making a huge claim; a named feud over credit | how a surveillance startup's system works |
| People | named lab leaders, researchers and testers on screen as cut-outs | one founder, one open-source developer |
| Cut pace | 11.0-13.3/min | 15.6/min |
| Text on screen | 57-72% of frames, 10.6-21.2 words per frame | 47%, 6.7 words per frame |
| What fills the frame (overview count, seen) | FluKUJyeYD8: ~24 of 40 frames documents, ~12 memes | ~13 documents, ~11 stock or product frames, ~5 icon diagrams, 3-4 memes |
| Body cut pattern | speeds up for the mess (FluKUJyeYD8) or slows for the statements (aspmNhKAFMc) | even 14-22 per minute |
| Loudness | −22.5 to −23.8 LUFS | −20.3 LUFS |
| Timbre | brightness 908 Hz, bass share 0.28-0.29 | 596 Hz, 0.50 |

Hypotheses:
1. **Topic and people beat pace.** The outliers cut slower than the control. A claim or a fight at a
   lab the audience already follows, with named people, is the likelier driver. Both outliers are
   also news-timed, so part of their views may come from the news event itself.
2. **Receipts.** The outliers show the source (the statement, the tweet, the benchmark table) in
   57-72% of frames. For a claim that is contested or hyped, seeing the document may be what makes
   a sarcastic narrator credible.
3. **An open loop about people.** aspmNhKAFMc names a phone call in its first 43 seconds and pays it
   off at 3:07. FluKUJyeYD8 promises to test an AGI claim and deflates it at 6:23. The control's
   loop ("how is this legal?") is answered in 30 seconds at 3:00-3:28.
4. **The audio difference is probably not causal.** The control is louder, darker and bassier; the
   cause is unknown (a bed, a mic chain, b-roll sound) and nothing ties it to views.

## Sound and music profile
- **Level:** −20.3 to −23.8 LUFS integrated (measured), 6-10 LU under the −14 reference. YouTube
  does not turn quiet uploads up (knowledge chapter), so these play soft (inferred). A new channel should
  still mix to −14 (knowledge chapter default).
- **Range:** 2.4-3.2 LU (measured), flatter than the documentary niche's 5.9-7.9 and close to the
  animated explainer pilot's 2.2. The voice is the whole mix (inferred).
- **Silence:** none in the body of any video (measured). This niche uses zero silence as a device;
  the only "pause" is a borrowed clip.
- **Clips as the dynamics:** the loudest windows in all three are borrowed clips with their own
  audio, 3-8 LU over the median (measured, matched to transcript). The chapter's "comic stingers"
  row for fast tech news fits, with the clip itself as the stinger (inferred).
- **Music:** no [music] tags in the outliers and one in the control; tempo readings jump by 60-100
  BPM between minutes (measured). Any bed is quiet and not beat-led (inferred). This matches the
  chapter's fast-tech-news row: "quiet driving bed or none under dense speech".
- **Cut sync:** lift 0.96-1.41 (measured). Nobody cuts to a beat. aspmNhKAFMc's 1.41 suggests cuts
  on word or hit transients (inferred).
- **Sponsor bed:** FluKUJyeYD8's bass share rises to 0.40-0.45 and brightness falls to 530-710 Hz
  during the ad (measured), so the ad likely has its own bed (inferred).

## Steal list
| Technique | Measured range | Works when | Backfires when | HyperFrames recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Screenshot receipts on black | text in 47-72% of frames, 6.7-21.2 words per frame | news, claims, disputes | the viewer can't read it in the hold (under ~1.5s for a sentence) | cards as divs on `#0b0b0b`, `rotate:±2deg`, drop shadow; enter `y:20→0, opacity:0→1` 200 ms `power3.out`; hold `max(1.5s, words×0.35s)` |
| Highlight bar on the spoken clause | 1 clause per card | the narrator paraphrases a document | the bar covers the whole paragraph | `<span>` with an orange `::before` bar, `scaleX:0→1` 250 ms `power2.out`, timed to the clause's first word |
| Sticker caption on the key noun | 1-3 per 30s in the hooks | naming, numbers, jokes | whole sentences as stickers | heavy condensed caps, `-webkit-text-stroke:6px #000`, `rotate:-8deg`; `scale:1.3→1` over 120 ms `expo.out` at the word's transcript time |
| Borrowed clip as the only loud moment | +3 to +8 LU over the median, 1-2 per video | a flat, fast narration that needs a reset | clips every 30s (the reset stops resetting) | clip at full level, narration track muted (not ducked) for the clip's `data-duration`; back to narration on a hard cut |
| Cold open ending on a fixed show line | 43-50s | a recurring series | one-off videos (the brand line means nothing yet) | one title-card sub-composition with a `date` variable; same line, same card every episode |
| Two-speed body | 13-15 cuts/min for context, 8-9 for statements | explainer then dispute | both halves are dispute | context track with 3-5s cards; statement track with 6-10s holds and a slow `y:-2%` drift |
| Pixel-block dissolve at topic turns | 1-3 per video after the hook | list-shaped news | every cut | 16×9 grid of divs over the outgoing frame, pre-seeded random `opacity:1→0` stagger over 250 ms, `steps(1)` |
| Short, self-aware sponsor | 13-43s, always at the end | loyal audiences | mid-video, or a read longer than the story's last act | sponsor as its own final sub-composition; one offer sticker and a pointer to the link |
